# The length-six theorem and proof chain

Author: MaxwellLaw. Mathematical/computational checkpoint: 2026-10-25.
The author, MaxwellLaw, used AI as an assistive tool.

**Theorem.** Define `M(n,k)=lcm(n+1,...,n+k)` for natural n and positive k.
For every natural n,m with `m>=n+6`,

`M(n,6) != M(m,6)`.

The written mathematical argument and exact arithmetic now establish this
fixed-length theorem without assuming Magma rank or full-basis flags, GRH,
analytic-rank conjectures, or an experimentally chosen search cutoff.
**There is no complete Lean theorem with this endpoint. The original
JSP-000550 / Erdős #677 for arbitrary positive k remains unresolved.**
No external referee approval or worldwide priority determination is claimed.

## Proof

Suppose the two six-term LCMs are equal in the stated domain. The
[product/LCM reduction](MATHEMATICS.md) gives odd integers
`X=2n+7`, `Y=2m+7` with `X>=7`, `Y-X>=12`, satisfying one of

`a*Phi(Y²)=b*Phi(X²)`, `Phi(T)=(T-1)(T-9)(T-25)`,

where `(a,b)` is `(1,2),(1,5),(1,10)` or `(2,5)`. The exact carry constraints
are retained in the Lean equivalence. We will exclude all four square-curve
candidates in the original domain, which is stronger than using only the
carry-compatible ones. The reduction includes n=0 and m=n+6.

The [explicit rational maps](../cas/CAS.md) send every such curve point to a
rational point P on the corresponding displayed elliptic curve. Their
identities and compositions are checked exactly. The original-domain image
is finite, has negative y, and has strictly negative inverse denominator;
no point at infinity or excluded chart denominator hides a target point.
These maps are not assumed to preserve integrality.

The [independent group certificate](../cas/descent/RANK_PROOF.md) proves that
all rational points on each elliptic curve are generated by the specified
three points. It proves torsion-freeness, full saturation using a global
minimum-height/index argument, and rank at most three using explicit cubic
number-field and local-3 descent certificates. The previous positive pairing
already gives rank at least three. External CAS routines discover witnesses;
the rank and generation conclusions use the written proofs and independently
replayable arithmetic rather than CAS completion flags.

Write P as `m1*P1+m2*P2+m3*P3`. The
[height bounds](../cas/height-bounds/README.md), correctly normalized
[effective elliptic-logarithm bound](../cas/global-bound/INITIAL_BOUND.md),
[certified logarithms](../cas/global-bound/REAL_LOGS.md), and
[lattice-distance reductions](../cas/global-bound/LATTICE.md) prove
`max|mi|<10^100` and then the respective closed bounds `60,60,62,64`.
The limiting algebraic point, real period coefficient, all denominators,
rounding errors and strict integer cutoffs are included in that argument.

The [complete residual check](../cas/global-bound/ENUMERATION.md) covers all
7,642,936 coefficient triples in those proved boxes. Two distinct complete
finite-field implementations, using disjoint auxiliary primes, recover the
same 46 remaining rational points and their inverse-map coordinates. The
only inverse pairs with both coordinates odd squares at least 49 yield
`(n,m)=(13,15),(5,6),(3,4)`. Their gaps are 2,1,1, so all overlap. Every
other residual point fails an original-domain square or chart condition.
None has `m>=n+6`, contradicting the assumed collision.

## What the formal proof covers

The Lean library proves the original definitions and reductions, carry/gcd
lemmas, integer height-certificate algebra, the universal integer lattice
argument, all 25 concrete lattice-distance certificates, conditional
real rounding-error transfer, and an abstract saturation bridge. The
[verification table](VERIFICATION.md) names their exact types and scopes.
There are 105 explicit audited declarations, using only standard logical axioms.

Canonical-height theory, David's theorem, elliptic integration, Kummer descent,
number-field completeness, actual finite reductions and the full residual
computation are documented mathematics with exact arithmetic outside Lean.
They are not imported as axioms. Completing a kernel-checked k=6 endpoint
requires formalizing these steps or replacing them with another complete
method. The all-length problem additionally needs a uniform argument or a
true disjoint equal-length counterexample; the fixed-length result is not an
original-problem award submission.
